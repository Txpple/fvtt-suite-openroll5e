# Session Scribe: example records

Two complete session records made by [fvtt-mcp-sessionscribe](https://github.com/Txpple/fvtt-mcp-sessionscribe)
for *The Broken Heart of Greenrest*, the D&D 5e campaign the scribe was proven on (played July to
September 2026, concluded with session 9). The campaign repo is private, so these are copies, shared by
its DM with everything in: the player recap, the combat report and both sets of GM notes, spoilers
included. Session 9 was made by the scribe from the night's Craig recording and the Foundry chat
log. Session 8 came from the same `session-scribe` skill the day before it moved out of
`fvtt-mcp-dnd5e` into its own server, and is the session the scribe then replayed end to end as its
proof. The illustrations were rendered by
[fvtt-mcp-imagegen](https://github.com/Txpple/fvtt-mcp-imagegen) from the party's portraits, and the
combat numbers were read from [Open Roll 5e: Battle Flow](https://github.com/Txpple/fvtt-mod-battleflow)'s
chat cards.

The PDFs are the illustrated versions and render in the browser here. What each file is:

- `recap.md`, `recap.html`, `recap.pdf`: the player recap, written from the public transcript alone, so
  it holds no whisper and no blind roll. The HTML is the email body, with its pictures in `img/`;
  `recap-print.html` is what the PDF prints from.
- `combat-stats.md`, `combat-log.html`, `combat-log.pdf`: the combat report, each fight by the numbers.
- `gm-notes-story.*`: the plot side of the GM's notes: what changed in the world, what was promised,
  what is now canon and what is left hanging.
- `gm-notes-mechanics.*`: the table side: bookkeeping to apply, automation that cost time, rulings to
  keep consistent, and what the players said.
- `img/`: the illustrations.

Not copied: the transcripts (the table's words, verbatim), the chat log and the recording metadata.
The full layout of a record is described in the scribe's README.
