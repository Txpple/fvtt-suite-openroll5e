# Session 9 — The Heart Knot

**2026-09-29 · 1h51m recorded · Gren, Jetten, Morgash, Invictus · all four at level 6 · the last session of the campaign**

---

## TL;DR

The Thornheart's first raising went to **Pudgy**. In the morning the Duskheart possessed Jetten and read
**Shar's last offer** aloud: drive Midnight into the heart knot, trap the Dreamer, and take five
resurrections home. Invictus wavered; the party talked him back ("It's not the same sword. Don't lie to
yourself"), and at dawn on the lakeshore **Lathander appeared in the fog** and told him to bury the sword.
Midnight is in the ground at the Wyrmwood. At Greenrest's edge the whole town shared the **fourth dream**:
the Dreamer forgave Corin and herself and asked to be freed. Oswin begged them not to; **Pip** changed his
mind. Three **Greenrest Mobs** came down on the square; Gren's *Hypnotic Pattern* froze one for the whole
fight, Jetten's *Entangle* pinned another, and the four hearts went into the knot one per round — the
Duskheart, the Graveheart, the Tideheart, and the Thornheart from the saddle of Invictus's celestial
steed. The Dreamer stepped out, took the heart back, thanked them, and was gone. The mist returned,
Greenrest asked them to leave, and Invictus said his name: **Thomas.** The DM then narrated the epilogue.

---

## 1 · A heartbeat for Pudgy

Still in the lair after Bramblemaw. Invictus read the Thornheart out loud (*once between dawns… a body dead
no more than ten days… raise dead*) and pressed it to Pudgy before the rest. Pudgy woke: *"What happened?
Oh, my goodness. Oh. Did we kill the dragon? Did we beat him?"* Gren told him Honeysuckle and Pinenut did
not make it and fed him nuts. Pudgy: *"At least they did not die in vain. Will you promise to bury them
outside, please, Master Gren?"* Long rest in the geased druid's makeshift camp south of the great hall
(Invictus +3 HP, Jetten +24 HP, Gren and Morgash hit dice back). Jetten doesn't sleep.

## 2 · The Duskheart speaks

Morning. Jetten's eyes glazed inky black; the DM showed Andrew the *Duskheart — Shar's Offer* journal
("Jetten — read this first", then the card; about two minutes of permission fiddling first). Jetten read it
aloud to Invictus, verbatim:

> Invictus. Killing my high priest was regrettable, but it is forgivable. My offer still stands. Take the
> heart shards and Midnight to the Dreamer's tree. Find the tree's heart knot and plunge the blade into it.
> Midnight will drain the power from the tree and the shards. The Dreamer will be trapped. Each shard can
> grant you a resurrection, and the heart of the tree will grant you a fifth. The shards will be dead, but
> Midnight will have the ability to give you your five resurrections. You will win. You will get your
> family back. The town will win. The Dreamer's summer will remain in Greenrest forever. I will win. My
> will shall be done. Think carefully, Invictus. This is my last offer.

Midnight glowed while he read, and stopped after. Jetten: *"The Duskheart took control. I don't remember
saying those words. I know I said them, but it wasn't me."* The DM then shared the card with everyone.

## 3 · The argument

Invictus, genuinely torn: *"What's one fae versus an entire town?"* — and either way the heart is gone and
his family stays dead. He admitted the pain is easy to bear now and everything is foggy: *"I think that's
what Shar does for her acolytes. I'm infected by her in some way. Takes the pain away from you."*

- Jetten: *"It sounds like a trap, Invictus. Like a bargain with the devil."* / *"It's the evil trying to
  work its way into you. Don't turn to it."*
- Morgash: *"It seems very obvious that you will think that it's the best thing to do if you are getting
  everything from this."* / *"You lost your bearings when you killed that guy."* / *"You lose your vow. And
  now you're completely lost, my friend."* / *"It's not the same sword. Don't lie to yourself."*
- Jetten reminded him the sword was First Light. Invictus: *"Oh, I don't even remember that."*
- Gren: Shar is the deepest of evils; keep Invictus away from the tree so he doesn't slay a demigoddess;
  *"You haven't done anything wrong… you don't need redemption, but if you do this you will need
  redemption."* Suggested atonement at a temple of Lathander.

Invictus: *"I have to rely on my friends here and say, I can't take her up on that offer. It just seems
like the evil thing to do."* Asked to go outside and pray.

## 4 · The Dawnfather in the fog

On the lakeshore at early morning, Invictus knelt with his psalter (a gift from a priest), recited psalms
to Lathander, held the sword up and begged him to release Shar's power. The fog curled away:

> *"You are not completely lost, my child, but the sword you wield must be left here. Bury it. First Light
> is gone."*

They dug a hole together and **buried Midnight**; the DM removed it from the sheet. Invictus had Gren make
Pudgy swear secrecy — *"What is? I don't know anything. Yes, sir."* Honeysuckle and Pinenut were buried by
a tree with acorns ("a place you can come say hi to the tree and the fairy"); Gren's two corpse items were
removed. Pudgy went ahead to tell the forest their deeds and promised to come to town.

A new weapon: Jetten stashed a **Scimitar**, Gren a Spear, Morgash the Ember-Touched Greatsword (two-handed —
taken back). Invictus took the scimitar (he keeps his shield). Leaving: *"I just feel like I hear that
buried sword calling to me and screaming. I have to get out of here before I lose my mind."*

## 5 · The fourth dream

Late morning; crossing Greenrest's limits, Jetten's moonstone glowed and all four fell into the dream. The
bare hill, the fog close, the tree — and the **whole town present in their nightclothes, no mist between
them**: Oswin, Selma, Enid, Ambrose, Warin, Pip. For the first time, the dryad looked at them:

> *"You've been here before, in the summer, at the fog, at the breaking. You are the ones holding my heart.
> I want you to know I forgive him. I forgive Corin. I know he chose to leave me. But he was right. I
> forgive him. And now, after centuries, I can finally forgive myself for holding on so long, for all of
> it. I'm asking you, begging you, free me. Bring the hearts to my tree. Find the heart knot. Place them in
> it, one by one, and I will absorb them. On the fourth, I shall be free. Please, help me. I've been
> trapped for centuries."*

As she faded she looked at Invictus and smiled a little. They woke at the town's edge. Invictus: *"My vote
is to go straight there and do it. I'm feeling a lot more moral clarity now… Hey, what was I thinking
before?"*

## 6 · The mayor in the road

Oswin met them in the road near the square with Pip, out of breath: *"I know what you're doing. I saw you
in the dream… Please don't do this. We'll do anything. You don't have to let her go. She's been here the
whole time. What's the difference?"* — a key to the city, live here as long as you want, free meals (Ambrose
already agreed). *"I'm fond of you all and that's what makes this so hard… the town will never prosper
again. The fog, I'm sure, will come back."* Invictus: *"It's just evil to keep her trapped there. You know
that."* Pip: *"I think he's right, Mayor. They need to do what they need to do."* Oswin: *"Oh, Pip, you're
right. Very well. I'll tell the guards to stand down. Do what you need to do. I'll go tell the elders."*

## 7 · The heart knot (combat, 5 rounds)

The DM showed the tree (the heart-knot illustration, Enid at the knot) and ruled: **one heart at a time;
placing one takes a full turn of concentration; interrupted, start over; absorbed at the placer's next
turn.** Nonlethal is melee-only. Three mobs came: *"Get them! They can't take our town away! Stop this!"*

Initiative: Gren 22 · Jetten 14 · The Whole Street 8 · Invictus 8 · Morgash 8 · Farmhands 2 · Torchbearers 1.

- **R1.** Gren: *Innate Sorcery* + *Hypnotic Pattern* on The Whole Street (a Careful Spell / tracker
  mix-up and a refund first) — they failed (12) and stayed hypnotized all fight; *Spellfire Burst*
  Bolstering Flames, 8 temp HP. Jetten: begins the **Duskheart**. Invictus: *Thaumaturgy* booming voice,
  Intimidation with advantage **20 vs DC 17** — the Farmhands pause. Morgash: Intimidation 15 + *Tactical
  Mind* 4 = **19** — the Torchbearers pause too. Neither mob attacked.
- **R2.** Gren: *Scorching Ray* into a wall as a warning, Intimidation 8 vs DC 21, Heroic Inspiration → 8.
  **Duskheart absorbed.** Jetten: *Entangle* on the Torchbearers (Str 5 — restrained). Invictus: hands up,
  "I refuse to attack", Intimidation 15 vs DC 25. Morgash: begins the **Graveheart**. Farmhands at Invictus:
  10, 12, 16 — all miss AC 22; cabbage 15 at Gren → *Shield* (AC 20), miss; tomato miss. Entangled crowd
  fails its escape (5); two thrown veg at Jetten with disadvantage, both miss.
- **R3.** Gren delays. Jetten: *Elementalism* (beckon air — shutters bang, nobody impressed); stands between
  mob and tree. Invictus: *Find Steed* — a celestial white horse; mounts. **Graveheart absorbed.** Morgash:
  Intimidation 10, fail. Gren (delayed): begins the **Tideheart**; tomato 8, cabbage 10 miss him. Farmhands
  at Jetten: 12, 10, 11 — all miss. Torchbearers (still pinned, escape 16 fails) at Morgash: pitchfork 13
  misses → **Riposte**, pike 21, **20 piercing** (push mastery, no effect on restrained); club 21 → 6, sword
  18 → 9 (3 + 6 after Heavy Armor Master); veg misses.
- **R4. Tideheart absorbed.** Gren: *"None shall pass!"*, Vesper Staff, nonlethal, 20 to hit, 3 to a
  farmhand's knee. Jetten: caltrops. Invictus: rides to the tree and leans from the saddle to press the
  **Thornheart** in — *"This is for you, and blot out all my iniquities, Lathander"* — Athletics **26** vs
  DC 15. Farmhands at Jetten: pitchfork 20 → 11, club 23 → 6, sword 9 miss, cabbage 20 → 7, tomato miss;
  concentration 12 ✓, 14 ✓, 5 → Heroic Inspiration 7 ✗ — **Entangle ends.** Torchbearers at Morgash:
  pitchfork 22 → 4 (1 after HAM), club 13 and sword 8 miss → **Riposte, 15**; tomato 25 hits Invictus
  **square in the eye** for 5 — Athletics 24, he keeps his seat.
- **R5.** Jetten: caltrops 1 to the Farmhands; recasts *Entangle* — Farmhands save (16, 18); the template also
  caught Gren (8, restrained) and Jetten (15). **Thornheart absorbed.**

## 8 · The passing

*"On the fourth heart, the knot opens and the dryad steps out of the trunk, holding the whole heart. She says
to you, 'It's stone. It has been stone in your hands for so long, warm and hard, and now it beats inside of
me again.' She closes her hand around her chest. The light goes out of the stone shards. Their edges lose
their shape. It grows soft and warm and wet… It wasn't a jewel at all. Now it's a heart. It's a plain red
ordinary meat beating in her hands."* She put the heart back, the mob started screaming, the tree went into
full bloom, and she disappeared. The mob walked away muttering and dejected. From far off: *"Thank you.
Thank you so much. I can never repay you."* Morgash: *"Yes, you can. Wait."*

Pudgy arrived: *"Oh, boss, I just got back. What happened?"* Gren gave him an acorn. Pudgy stays by Gren
from here on — "your little best friend familiar from all times to come."

## 9 · The mist comes home

The mists rolled slowly back into town. Commoners looked at them sad and dejected and would not speak to
them; the elders understood but could never forgive; Oswin: *"Please, please just go."* Then Invictus came
to and said: **"Thomas. My name is Thomas."**

## 10 · Epilogue (the DM, at the table)

- **Greenrest.** Leaves fall in the square for the first time in living memory; the children have never seen
  it; Oswin watches from the steps of the Mayor's Tower. A few families take the Trade Way south for the
  winter. At a sick child's bedside Enid prays, and for the first time in Greenrest's history something
  answers — Chauntea has forgiven the town. Harvests smaller but real; a healer of their own.
- **Morgash.** The gems were the big score — retirement. Warin asks him to hold the gate at Hedgewatch; he
  stays: *"I feel responsible."* In time forgiven; Oswin always stands him breakfast and dinner; a permanent
  room at the Full Larder.
- **Jetten.** One evening the moonstone glows green for the last time; a real dream of his sister, sent by the
  Dreamer; he says goodbye; in the morning the stone is milky white. Stays friends with Thomas, comes and goes.
- **Thomas.** A month later in Daggerford he mends things with the temple of Lathander, visits the five
  graves, reads every name aloud and then his own; stays until dark; the grief ends. Becomes a paladin again
  and goes wandering with Jetten for a few years ("like Fafhrd and the Grey Mouser"), slaying evil.
- **Gren.** Every squirrel knows Gren the Mighty freed the Green Lady; the warren sends for him; a feast. He
  plants the Everseed Acorn in the square beside the old tree and stays — the squirrel cult with Pudgy as
  prophet, "reluctantly tolerate Morgash." The acorn grows into a young oak that drops its leaves each
  autumn. **Gren, Morgash and Pudgy: the Three Amigos.** Gren's old debt to Morgash is paid in acorns by the
  bedside every morning; Morgash steps on them half the time.

The table then talked about the next campaign (see the mechanics notes).
