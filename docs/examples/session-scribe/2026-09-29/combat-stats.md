**The heart clock.** Duskheart (Jetten) placed R1 → absorbed R2 · Graveheart (Morgash) R2 → R3 ·
Tideheart (Gren, delayed) R3 → R4 · Thornheart (Invictus, from the saddle, Athletics 26 vs DC 15) R4 → R5.
No placement was ever interrupted.

**Luck or balance?** Mild cold dice on the mobs, not a balance problem. The mobs attack at +6. Across the
27 attacks they made the observed hit rate was 30% (8/27) against party ACs of 15–22; against the mix of
targets they actually swung at, ~40% was the expectation. Three of the Farmhands' attacks went into
Invictus's AC 22 (all missed), and 1 of their 17 rolls was a natural 1. The DM called it at the table:
*"They all rolled pathetically."* The real reason the fight was cheap is the next section: one mob never
acted and a second spent three rounds pinned.
