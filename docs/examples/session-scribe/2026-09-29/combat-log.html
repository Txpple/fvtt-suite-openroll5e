3 · What the buffs and features actually bought

Every spell, maneuver and feature that touched a number, and what it produced. Ranked by how much it moved the fight.

The big three

  • Hypnotic Pattern was the fight. The Whole Street failed its save on a 12 and stood slack for all five rounds: about 25 attacks never made. At the night's mob rate (39 damage from 27 attacks, 1.44 per attack) that is roughly 36 damage prevented, and one of three crowds that could never reach the knot.
  • Riposte was the party's damage. 20 and 15 off two missed pitchforks: 35 of the party's 39. The pike's Push mastery rode both and did nothing to a restrained crowd.
  • Entangle bought three rounds of distance. The Torchbearers were restrained R2–R4 and failed three escape checks. It held two concentration saves (12, 14) and broke on the third (5, then Heroic Inspiration 7, vs DC 10). The R5 recast was shrugged off (16, 18) and also caught Gren and Jetten in its template.
