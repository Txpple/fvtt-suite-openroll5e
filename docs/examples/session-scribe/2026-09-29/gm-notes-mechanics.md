## 3 · Rulings to keep consistent

- **Placing a heart** = a full turn of concentration at the knot; interrupted = start over; one at a time;
  absorbed at the start of the placer's next turn.
- **Nonlethal**: melee only; same damage; flavour (flat of the blade).
- **Intimidating a mob**: DC 17, rising to 21 and then 25 as the crowd heated up. *Thaumaturgy*'s booming
  voice grants advantage. A success made that mob pause and lose its round.
- **Single-target control on a mob** (Hold Person): affects one person in the crowd — not worth it. Area
  spells work normally.
- **Find Steed**: instantaneous; mounting takes the action; leaning from the saddle to the knot = Athletics
  DC 15; taking a hit while leaning = Athletics DC 15 to stay seated.
- **The Thornheart's Raise Dead** was cast as an action, not the spell's hour.
- **Caltrops vs a mob**: a normal 5-ft square even though the mob is bigger.
