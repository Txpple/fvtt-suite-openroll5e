Combat Log — The Wyrmwood

22 September 2026 · 3 combats · folded from 166 Battle Flow stamped messages of 485

Three fights in Bramblemaw's Lair: a two-round Shambling Mound, a two-round druid and giant crocodile, and a four-round adult green dragon. The party dealt 584 and took 269. Jetten and Invictus each hit 0 twice; nobody died. All three squirrel allies died to the dragon's first breath.

Bramblemaw died with 3 hit points to spare — 292 damage into a 289 HP dragon. He had about 12 left when Gren's Fireball went off, and its saved half (15) was enough. Take away any one of the three biggest contributors below and the dragon lives: Topple's advantage (65), Morgash's second Action Surge (29), or the wand's Magic Missile (22).

4 · Bramblemaw — 4 rounds

What the dragon did. Two breaths (32, 30) — the first killed all three squirrels and put Jetten to 14; three Mind Spikes (19, 15, 8), two of which dropped a PC; five Pounces; three Noxious Miasmas; and two Legendary Resistances, spent on Invictus's shield bash and Gren's Slow. His Rends found Invictus's AC 22 twice in six tries.

Raised by Gren's Healing Word twice, Selma's Superior Healing (27, Morgash → Invictus), and 2 points of Lay on Hands (Invictus → Jetten).

A deadly fight, run straight, won by 3 HP — and the dice were unkind to two of the four. Invictus failed every save he rolled against the dragon: four Con saves at +4 vs DC 16 (55% to fail each) and one Wis at +6 (45%) — about 4%, 1 in 24, one of them a natural 1. Jetten failed all three Mind Spike saves with advantage (36% to fail each) — about 5%, 1 in 21.

The other way: the breath recharged at the top of round 3 but went unchecked until after the dragon's action, so the second breath came a round late; and two of his three Legendary Resistances went on a shield bash and Slow rather than on the two topples that actually killed him. An adult green dragon into four level-6 PCs is meant to be deadly, and it played that way. Not a balance problem.

5 · What the buffs and features actually bought

Every spell, mastery and maneuver that touched a number tonight, and what it produced, ranked by how much it moved the fights.

The winners

  • Topple, 4 for 4. Two prone on the Mound, two on the dragon, none resisted. On Bramblemaw the advantage converted five misses (totals on the low die: 18, 16, 13, 12, 10 vs AC 19) into 65 damage — the single biggest thing any feature did tonight.
  • Potions of Poison Resistance, 800 gp for ~75 HP. The best money the party has spent. The lesson in it: Jetten drank Antitoxin, not resistance, before the doors. The first breath took him 46 → 14 and the Mind Spike finished him. With the potion down first he takes 16, sits on ~28 after the Miasma, and survives the spike on ~9.
  • Death Armor killed a giant crocodile by itself (the 6 on its second bite) and put 10 more into the dragon. Its death-save advantage didn't save the one roll it got (8).
  • Selma's Superior Healing — the price of a finger — took Invictus from 0 to 27 in round 4, and his first act back up was 2 points of Lay on Hands that stood Jetten up. Jetten then saved the next Miasma on a 20 and hit for 15.
  • Bless, one flip: Jetten 18 → 19 against AC 19, 18 damage.

The duds, named as plainly

  • Sap: nine applications, no outcome changed. Both dragon Rends it put at disadvantage (13, 12) would have missed AC 22 on the higher die anyway (16, 14).
  • Aura of Protection (new at 6): no save flipped. Morgash passed with room to spare; Invictus's closest miss was 15 vs 16 with the +2 already in; Jetten missed three of his four aura saves by 3 or more.
  • Antitoxin ×3: nothing. Nothing in the lair imposed Poisoned — the Miasma takes AC.
  • Counterspell: the dragon saved, 22 vs DC 18. Heroic Inspiration: Morgash's reroll went 17 → 15, still a miss.
  • Potions of Fire Breath, 600 gp for 8 damage. Invictus exhaled once (17, saved to 8). Morgash drank his and never exhaled; Jetten never drank his.
  • Innate Sorcery: +1 DC on a Slow the dragon failed by 6; no spell attacks in its minute. Death Ward never triggered; Gren bottomed out at 8.
