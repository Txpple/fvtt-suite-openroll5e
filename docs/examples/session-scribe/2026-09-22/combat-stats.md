## 7 · Luck, and the shape of it

**A deadly fight, run straight, won by 3 HP — and the dice were not kind to two of the four.**

- **Invictus failed every save he rolled against the dragon** — four Con (DC 16 at +4, 55% to fail
  each) and one Wis vs *Mind Spike* (+6, 45%): **~4%, about 1 in 24.** One of those was a nat 1.
- **Jetten failed all three *Mind Spike* saves — with advantage** (Duskheart; +3 needs 13 on the
  better die, 36% to fail each): **~5%, about 1 in 21.**
- The other way: the dragon's breath **recharged at the start of round 3 but went unchecked until
  after his action** (*"should have done that before"*), so the second breath landed in round 4
  instead of round 3. And he spent two of his three Legendary Resistances on a shield bash and
  *Slow* rather than on Morgash's two topples, which were what actually killed him.

Not a balance problem. An adult green dragon into four level-6 PCs is meant to be deadly, and it
played that way: two PCs down twice, the party at 3 HP of margin.

**Advantage economy:** Morgash ×9, Jetten ×7, Invictus ×2, Gren ×2; disadvantage: Shambling Mound
×2, Bramblemaw ×2, Invictus ×1, Gren ×1.
