# Session 8 — The Wyrmwood

**2026-09-22 · 2h59m recorded · Gren, Jetten, Morgash, Invictus · all four at level 6**

---

## TL;DR

The third dream broke the whole town open. Every soul in Greenrest dreamed the dryad's
last night with the raven-haired man — his refusal, her waiting, the heart torn in four — and
woke up at the same moment. By breakfast Oswin had seen the party standing on the hill, Pip
had drawn the four colours in berry juice, the children had invented a game called *The
Breaking*, and Selma's tonic had stopped working. The party bought poison resistance and fire
breath, followed Sera's map to a half-drowned temple in a forest lake, and went in with three
squirrels who worship Gren. They cut through a Shambling Mound, a geased druid and a giant
crocodile, and then fought **Bramblemaw** across his flooded hall for four rounds. His first
breath killed **Pudgy, Honeysuckle and Pinenut**; Jetten and Invictus each went down twice;
and Gren ended it with a Careful Fireball *"for Honeysuckle, Pinenut and Pudgy."* Invictus
picked **the Thornheart** off the hoard. All four pieces are in the party's hands. The DM called
the next session the last.

---

## 1 · The third dream

Housekeeping first: the DM paid Morgash the Heroic Inspiration owed from the session-7 grapple
argument, and flagged Invictus's new **Aura of Protection** (+2 saves, 10 ft).

The party took the inn at dusk — beer on Ambrose, as always, and the common room staring at
them the way people stare at celebrities without coming over. Gren complained that Jetten had
taken his room and threatened to haunt it.

**Dream 3 — the refusal.** Jetten's moonstone glowed bright green as they slept. Back on the
hillside in the mist — but this time **they were not the only ones there**: other hazy figures
stood in the fog around them. The dryad and the raven-haired man were arguing, and she was
losing it:

> *"Stay — stay, and I'll make it summer for you forever. Be as I am. Never end."*

> *"If you take the ending out of me — if you take away my mortality — there's nothing left
> that you love. A life isn't for keeping, my dear. It's for spending. Let me go and spend
> mine."*

He turned his back and went down the hill. She followed him exactly as far as a line she could
not cross, and watched the fog take him. Then time ran fast: real autumns, light winters, brief
snow on the orchard, and her waiting at the fog-line every evening for a man who never came
back. When she finally gave up she tore her heart out and ripped it into four — **red, blue,
green, and a blackish purple** — and threw the pieces out in four directions. The shock blew
the mist off the valley in a ring, all at once. Where she had stood, she folded down into a
tree.

## 2 · The morning — Invictus wavers

Invictus opened the day with a confession: *"I'm feeling like this town is deriving some major
benefit from this dryad being pulled up in that tree, and I'm afraid that if we set her free,
it'll destroy this town… I just don't seem to have my same moral compass anymore… Maybe she
deserves to be there."* Jetten: *"It's not right to let her remain enslaved, Invictus."* Gren
pointed out it was self-inflicted — and that the man had said life is for spending. Invictus
let it drop, for now.

## 3 · The town that woke up

**Oswin** met them downstairs, exhausted and electrified: *"I saw you — you were on the hill."*
All four of them, and half the town saw them too. The waitress had dreamed it and could not
sleep — *"it's almost like the explosion made its way into my mind."* Ambrose set down three
orange juices with his hands shaking and walked back to the bar without a word.

Invictus gave the mayor the careful version: a vision of something long ago, and the party's
suspicion that it has to do with the town's endless summer. Oswin got there on his own: *"She
was offering him eternal summer, eternal life, and he refused. It seems we might be the
beneficiaries of that."* He asked whether they had seen these gems before; Invictus deflected.
He will call **a meeting of the elders** today.

Then Invictus told him about the **Temple of Shar** (first Oswin had heard of Sharrans out
here — *"I hope you dealt with them"*) and that its acolytes were vehemently against freeing
the dryad. Gren added that the body-part trader was a regular here — and Morgash **put the
finger on the breakfast table.** *"Sir, there's children here… I appreciate what you've done,
but that's pretty disgusting."* Back in the pack it went. The Shar–Selûne enmity was restated
(the dryad is a demigoddess and an ally of Selûne), and Invictus mentioned that Jetten is a
devotee of Selûne.

Asked where they were going next, the party told him: a green dragon. Oswin hoped it was an
evil one and not a gold or silver; Gren: *"If it's a good dragon, it will talk, and it will have
a cup of tea."* Oswin recalled an old lake where Sera's map points.

**Pooled dragon lore** (a group Arcana check that needed 50: Jetten 2, Invictus 12, Gren 8,
Morgash 17 + Tactical Mind = 24 — short): green dragons breathe poison, charm and corrupt,
love treasure, live in forests, and are semi-amphibious.

**Pip** was outside, drawing four wedges in the dirt in berry juice — red, blue, purple,
green: *"Those are the gems that I dreamed about. Everybody sees them. I haven't had dreams in
so long."* Across the field the children were playing a game they had invented that morning,
**The Breaking**: one child falls down and four run away in four directions, each in a red,
green, blue or purple shirt. The adults were not happy about it.

## 4 · Selma, and a finger

**Selma** was not good. Her potions were not working; she was trying a double dose; and she
had dreamed too — *"I saw the mist. I saw it roll out like a tide. I've never dreamed like this
in so long… The whole town is dreaming again."*

Shopping:
- **Sold** the rest of the temple loot to her: gold disc 200, violet stone 150, the Smiling
  Merchant portrait 70, gold tesserae 100, cave-bloom draught 90, star-chart folio 75, sanctuary
  velvet 60 (745 gp).
- **Four Potions of Poison Resistance** (800 gp from the stash). Jetten later sold one spare
  back for 100.
- **Potions of Fire Breath** for Invictus, Jetten and Morgash (200 gp each), and Potions of
  Healing for Invictus and Jetten.
- Invictus swapped *Aid* for *Protection from Poison* in his prepared list.

Then **Morgash offered Selma "a piece of jewelry"** — the signet ring, still on Veck's finger.
She dropped it. *"That's his finger? Is he still alive?"* — *"He was already dead. I didn't kill
him. I just took the finger so you could rest assured he's not coming back."* Selma thanked
him, and gave him a **Potion of Superior Healing** on the house. Gren muttered about aiding and
abetting on the way out. (That potion saved Invictus's life two hours later.)

## 5 · The Wyrmwood, and the squirrels

Following Sera's map southwest: a medium forest lake, and a temple sunk half into it where the
water rose over it decades ago. Gren cast *Speak with Animals*, and three squirrels came out to
meet him — **Pudgy** from the Temple of Shar, with **Longshadow's peryton feather** fixed behind
his head, and his friends **Pinenut** (darker) and **Honeysuckle** (paler):

> *"Oh, it's the mighty warlord of the wood! All of the squirrels throughout the entire forest
> know of the great Gren."*

Gren demanded proper obeisance; all three bowed, Pudgy's feather dipping. Their intelligence:
*the big green one sleeps at the far end on the shiny warm thing; the water's bad, don't drink
it.* They volunteered to come in and guard him.

## 6 · The drowned temple

Honeysuckle hopscotched across the stepping-stones and showed them a secret door in a fountain
face; the doors beyond were locked (*"last time it was open — now it's shut"*) and nobody had
thieves' tools. Jetten (Perception 20) found a hand-engraved secret door onto a collapsed tunnel
— and **Pudgy squeezed through the rubble** and came back forty minutes later: *"There's a
hallway on the other side. You are a mighty Gren. How did you know?"*

## 7 · The Shambling Mound

A second engraved door (Morgash, Perception 22) opened on a large creature that came straight
for them. Gren *Bless*ed the three melee fighters and backed off; Invictus cast *Death Armor* on
himself; Jetten put *Hunter's Mark* on it and two arrows in (14, 12). Morgash dashed in on
*Adrenaline Rush* and toppled it twice with the Maul of Momentum (14 vs 15, then 5 vs 15),
answering a missed tendril with a *Riposte* for 21. It landed one tendril on him (16, cut to 13
by Heavy Armor Master). Gren lit Invictus up with *Light* — *"may the light guide [his] path."*
Invictus shield-bashed and hit twice (13, 17), and **Jetten's shortsword finished it** — it
crumbled at his feet. *"It was a mound of shambling vegetables."*

## 8 · The druid and the crocodile

The next doors opened on **a horned, tiefling-looking druid** — *"Intruders!"* — and a **giant
crocodile** too big for the doorway. Jetten opened on the druid (8, 10). Gren's *Sorcerous
Burst* missed, and **Pudgy giggled** — *"Oh! Warlord Gren. Sorry!"*

The crocodile bit Invictus for 24 and grappled him; *Death Armor* bit it back for 5. Invictus,
in its jaws, **shield-bashed it prone** and hit twice (15, 17). The druid's eyes glowed an
unearthly green — *"Must serve the master"* — and he cast *Spike Growth*. Morgash waded through
the thorns and hammered the crocodile (7, 14). Jetten **crit the druid** (nat 20: 33, with
*Dreadful Strike*); he held concentration on an 18 and went down to the next arrow (13). Gren's
*Fire Bolt* came out a nat 20 on a disadvantage roll — both dice 20 — for 23. The crocodile bit
Invictus a second time (14) and **died on his *Death Armor*.** *"Ending combat with one wounded
paladin."*

Pudgy scratched the next locked door open. Gren cast *Death Ward* on himself from the
Tideheart; healing went round (Healing Word, Second Wind, two potions, Cure Wounds, Lay on
Hands). A maze of dead-end halls, a room of pulleys — one lever rusted solid, two freshly oiled
— and a pull that made *a promising sound*: Pinenut reported the bar across the dragon's doors
was gone. Poison Resistance and Antitoxin down; Invictus drank his Fire Breath. The squirrels:
*"Master Gren, be careful! That's where we saw the dragon last time… we'll be right behind
you, sir."*

## 9 · Bramblemaw (4 rounds)

Both doors swung open on a long bridge-like causeway across the water, giant crocodiles
drifting uninterested below, and the dragon resting at the far end. He spoke in Draconic —
*"Come for my treasure, eh? You'll die instead."* The DM, out of character: *"It's basically the
end of the campaign. I'm not gonna give any mercy — no DM fiat to save you either."*

**Round 1.** Bramblemaw won initiative (21), came 25 feet up the causeway and breathed:
**32 poison, DC 16 Con.** **Pinenut (1), Honeysuckle (4) and Pudgy (9) all failed — all three
squirrels died.** Jetten failed (15) and took the full 32 — no potion yet — and lost *Hunter's
Mark*; Invictus failed (16 after resistance); Gren saved with the Tideheart's advantage (8);
Morgash saved (16). The dragon flew back to perch. Morgash drank his resistance, then went to
Sera's longbow: a miss turned into a hit by *Precision Attack* (16 + 8 = 24), then another hit.
Legendary action: *Noxious Miasma* (Jetten failed, AC down). Invictus stepped into the water,
the crocodiles noticed, and he **Misty Stepped straight in front of the dragon** — Midnight 15,
**shield bash, Bramblemaw prone**, Midnight again at advantage for 22. (No smite: the bonus
action was spent.) *Pounce* — the Rend at disadvantage from *Sap* — missed. Jetten 16, then a
nat 1. **Legendary *Mind Spike* on Jetten, 19 psychic — Jetten down.** Gren brought him up with
*Healing Word* (9), placed the spell with an Arcana check, and recast *Bless*.

**Round 2.** Breath didn't recharge. Two Rends at Invictus: one hit (13, bloodied; *Death
Armor* 5), one miss. The dragon flew; Invictus's opportunity attack hit (15) and his shield bash
floored it — **Bramblemaw burned a Legendary Resistance** to stay up. Morgash: two longbow
misses, Heroic Inspiration spent on one, still a miss. *Noxious Miasma* on the two fighters.
Invictus: javelins (5, miss) and **fire breath, 17 (halved on the save)**. Jetten 18 (with
*Bless* turning a 19 into exactly AC 19), a miss, a healing potion, and a shout in Draconic —
*"Stop with the tricks, dragon! Come and fight! What's your name, dragon?"* No answer. Pounce
missed Invictus again (*Sap*). Gren: *Innate Sorcery* + Careful *Slow* — Bramblemaw failed and
**burned a second Legendary Resistance.**

**Round 3.** Rend on Invictus (14; *Death Armor* 5), a miss; breath recharged. **Morgash**:
miss, hit 13 and **topple — prone** (9 vs 15), then *Action Surge*: 13, 12, both at advantage.
*"He's looking bloodied."* **Legendary *Mind Spike* on Invictus — 15, down**, and a failed death
save (8, even with *Death Armor*'s advantage). Jetten: shortsword miss, Goldthorn 11, a potion;
Pounce hit Jetten (12). **Gren**: *Healing Word* on Invictus (11 — up), then **five darts of
*Magic Missile* from the wand, 22**, and a *Counterspell* on the dragon's next *Mind Spike* at
DC 18 — Bramblemaw rolled a 22. Jetten took 8 and stayed up.

**Round 4.** Bramblemaw moved south through opportunity attacks (Morgash's pike 7; Goldthorn
missed) and **breathed again: 30.** Jetten failed (15 after resistance) — **down**; Invictus
failed (15) — **down**; Morgash saved. **Morgash poured Selma's Superior Healing into Invictus:
27.** Then maul 13 and **topple again** (10 vs 15), 11, and a second *Action Surge* — the maul
*sizzling with light* — 15 and 14. Pounce and Rend on Gren (13; he held *Slow*). Invictus, on
his feet: **two points of *Lay on Hands* to get Jetten up**, then two javelins (6, and a crit
for 7). *Noxious Miasma*: Jetten saved on a 20 — *"Those two hit points are paying off."*
Jetten: shortsword 8 (*Vex*), Goldthorn 7, a miss. Last legendary: **Pounce, a crit on Gren** —
AC 29, and 16 damage off two twos, leaving him at 8.

Gren, Careful Spell: **Fireball — *"This is for Honeysuckle, Pinenut and Pudgy, you son of
a—"*** 30 fire. Bramblemaw made the save and took 15. **It was enough. The Fireball slew
Bramblemaw.** A giant crocodile caught in the blast took 30; without the dragon's hold over them,
the crocodiles swam off into the depths. *"That was a good fight. It was very close. Good
job."*

## 10 · The Thornheart, and the hoard

A short rest in the room where the dragon's thralls slept — old druidic gear. Gren's Pearl of
Power. Behind a door to the north, **a glowing green heart.** **Invictus took it.**

> *The Thornheart.* A quarter of a heart, turned to crystal: venom green, bright and vivid and
> faintly poisonous-looking… It is warm — warmer than the air — and it beats, slowly, faintly…
> It is the exact green of the dragon that hoards it, which is either a coincidence or isn't —
> and the Wyrmwood has grown briar around it the way a body grows scar tissue.
> *The vision:* a beautiful green woman, glowing white, leaves crowning her head, weeping. She
> closes her hands over her own chest, and her heart bursts… Where she stood, a tree: grey,
> leafless, asleep. A single faint heartbeat, and it is over.
> Advantage on Dexterity saves; once between dawns, pressed to a body dead no more than ten
> days, it casts *raise dead*.

Gren immediately: *"Let's go raise Pudgy! … Pudgy's my only worshipper. He's like the head
priest."* Invictus asked which heart would bring back his family; the DM: *"That's when it's
combined. You get four true resurrections."* — *"Which you can do now. You have all four. You
just don't know how."* Gren's Arcana (16 vs 15): **put them together at the source** — at the
tree.

**The hoard** (rolled straight off the DMG tables, the DM said — nothing handpicked): Staff of
Withering, Decanter of Endless Water, Driftglobe, Rival Coin (Tiamat side up), and ~10,700 gp in
gems — three black opals, three blue sapphires, two star rubies, a fire opal, a yellow sapphire,
an aquamarine, a jade bead and a crimson coral.

The DM: *"The last session, you're going to have to make a choice what you want to do with the
heart."* Invictus: **"I already know the answer, because this sword has been whispering to
me."** The DM, on Pudgy: *"I'm very disappointed you're not going to raise Pudgy, because he was
my little buddy."*

## Where things stand

- **All four hearts:** Graveheart (Morgash), Tideheart (Gren), Duskheart (Jetten), **Thornheart
  (Invictus)**. Gren's read: they can be joined at the source — the tree.
- **Next session is the last.** The choice is what to do with the heart.
- **Invictus's compass has turned.** He argued at breakfast that the dryad may deserve her tree;
  he says Midnight has been whispering the answer to him.
- **Pudgy, Honeysuckle and Pinenut are dead** in Bramblemaw's hall. The Thornheart can raise one
  of them tomorrow at dawn; nobody has decided.
- **Greenrest is awake.** The whole town dreamed the breaking; the elders are meeting; the
  children play The Breaking; Selma's tonic has failed.
- Oswin now knows about the Temple of Shar and that the body-part trader is dead. He does not yet
  know about the hearts by name.
